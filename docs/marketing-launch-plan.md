# SaferSay Marketing Launch Plan

Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Positioning

SaferSay is the confidential employee survey tool for 10-60 person companies where founders need honest feedback without buying an enterprise HR platform.

Use this framing externally:

> Your team may not tell you the truth directly. SaferSay helps small-company founders hear what people are actually saying, without exposing individual employees or buying an enterprise HR suite.

Avoid:

- Broad "employee engagement" category language.
- "AI HR platform" as the lead claim.
- Public self-serve launch language before the trust and SaaS gates are complete.

## Stage 1: Design-Partner Launch

Goal: run 3-5 real company survey cycles before public launch.

Ideal buyers:

- Founder, operator, or HR lead at a 10-60 person company.
- Recently grew quickly, lost employees, went remote/hybrid, raised funding, restructured, or suspects the team mood has changed.
- Can act without complex procurement, union, public-sector, or heavy legal review.

Pilot offer:

- Confidential employee survey setup.
- Secure invite links.
- k-protected aggregate reporting.
- Plain-English report and one recommended next action.
- Simple per-cycle pilot pricing.
- Optional report retention for trend lines.

Pilot success metrics:

- 3-5 pilots launched.
- Survey launched within 48 hours after customer data is ready.
- Each survey reaches the minimum safe response threshold.
- At least 2 founders say the report changed what they decided to do.
- At least 1-2 customers agree to be referenced anonymously or publicly.
- Pricing objections are recorded verbatim.

## Outreach Copy

### Warm Founder DM

Hi [Name] — I am running a small number of founder-led pilots for SaferSay, a confidential employee survey tool for 10-60 person teams.

It is for the moments where something feels off but people may not say it directly: recent growth, unexpected resignations, remote-work drift, a raise, or a restructure.

The pilot is simple: we help you run one confidential survey, collect enough responses to unlock a protected aggregate report, and identify one concrete action to take.

Would it be useful to run this with your team or compare notes for 20 minutes?

### Fractional HR / Advisor DM

Hi [Name] — I am looking for 3-5 design partners for SaferSay, a confidential employee survey product for 10-60 person companies.

The wedge is small teams that need honest feedback but do not want Culture Amp/Lattice-style annual HR software. Employees answer through confidential links, and founders only see grouped reporting after the safe threshold is met.

If you work with founders who recently grew, lost people, or are about to restructure, I would value an intro.

### LinkedIn Launch Post

Small-company founders often learn about team problems too late.

By the time two people quit, the signal was probably already there. The problem is that employees do not always feel safe saying the real thing directly to the founder.

I am opening a small number of design-partner pilots for SaferSay: confidential employee surveys for 10-60 person companies.

The goal is not another enterprise engagement dashboard. It is one fast, confidential survey, a protected aggregate report, and one clear action the founder can take next.

If you run or advise a small team where something feels off, I would like to talk.

## Stage 2: Public Launch Readiness

Do not run a broad Product Hunt-style public launch until these gates are complete:

- Real tenant isolation and security hardening.
- Google and Microsoft sign-in.
- Stripe live payments.
- Resend production email/domain setup.
- Public landing page with a self-serve sample path.
- Clear privacy, DPA, and confidentiality pages.
- Exportable or shareable report loop.

Public launch promise:

> SaferSay helps small-company founders run confidential employee surveys and get a board-ready report without an HR platform.

## Product Hunt Assets

Tagline:

> Confidential employee surveys for small teams.

Positioning:

> The confidential survey tool for founders who need the truth before problems become resignations.

Founder comment structure:

1. Name the problem: small teams need honest feedback, but direct feedback to founders is hard.
2. Explain why enterprise HR tools are wrong for this buyer: heavy, annual, per-seat, and built for bigger companies.
3. Explain the trust mechanism: employees answer confidentially; founders see aggregate reports only after safe thresholds are met.
4. Show proof from pilots: one anonymized customer moment or quote.
5. Invite action: try the sample survey path or join the next pilot batch.

Required assets before launch:

- 60-90 second demo video.
- Screenshot of the landing page.
- Screenshot of survey setup.
- Screenshot of employee confidentiality screen.
- Screenshot of protected report before k threshold.
- Screenshot of unlocked aggregate report.
- Pilot quote or mini-case study.

Launch-day checklist:

- Publish Product Hunt page.
- Post founder story on LinkedIn.
- Email warm network with the launch link.
- Post in founder/operator communities where self-promotion is allowed.
- Reply to every Product Hunt comment with a specific answer.
- DM qualified upvoters/commenters asking who they know at a 10-60 person company.
- Publish a follow-up post with one anonymized pilot lesson.

## Content Plan

Use specific founder moments as the content engine.

Initial post topics:

- Why your 20-person team stops telling you the truth.
- Anonymous surveys are not enough.
- The first survey after two unexpected resignations.
- Culture Amp is great. It is not built for a 25-person startup.
- What a founder should ask before a restructure.
- Why reports, not dashboards, are the viral loop for employee feedback.

Lead magnets:

- First confidential survey template for 10-60 person teams.
- Founder checklist: are employees safe to be honest?
- Pilot privacy notice template.

## Default Weekly Cadence

- Monday: founder-moment post.
- Tuesday: 10 targeted founder DMs.
- Wednesday: advisor/fractional HR intro requests.
- Thursday: proof post from pilot learning.
- Friday: follow-up with every open pilot conversation.

Track weekly:

- Founder conversations started.
- Qualified pilot calls booked.
- Pilots launched.
- Surveys reaching threshold.
- Reports reviewed with founder.
- Paid conversions or explicit pricing objections.
