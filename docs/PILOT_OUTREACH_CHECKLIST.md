# Pilot Outreach Checklist

Use this for controlled design-partner outreach. Do not position SaferSay as fully self-serve production software yet.

## Ideal First Customers

- 10-60 employees
- founder or HR lead can act quickly
- runs employee surveys once or twice a year
- dislikes annual per-seat HR contracts
- wants confidential, plain-English feedback
- comfortable with founder-led setup

Avoid first:

- regulated/public-sector buyers
- unions/works councils
- complex procurement
- companies needing HRIS integrations on day one
- companies asking for legal anonymity guarantees beyond the product controls

## Offer

SaferSay pilot includes:

- confidential employee survey setup
- secure invite links
- k-enforced aggregate reporting
- CSV export
- AI summary based on group scores only
- one next-action recommendation
- optional report retention for trend lines

## Qualification Questions

1. How many employees would be invited?
2. When was the last employee survey?
3. What happened after the last survey?
4. Who needs to see results?
5. Are there any legal, union, or works-council constraints?
6. Do you need Google/Microsoft sign-in?
7. Can you use a pilot privacy notice and DPA outline, subject to review?

## Pilot Success Criteria

- survey launched within 48 hours of customer data being ready
- at least k responses collected
- founder/HR can explain what the report says
- one concrete action is committed
- customer agrees the pricing model is clearer than annual per-seat tools

## Do Not Promise Yet

- full self-serve onboarding
- full legal review
- HRIS sync
- manager/team segmented reports
- benchmark database
- anonymous open-text grievance handling
- live production SLA
